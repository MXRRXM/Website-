Immersive Classroom & AI for Education

01. The Modern Classroom, Reimagined

Manifest XR helps schools and universities build immersive learning environments that make complex subjects easier to understand, more engaging to teach, and more memorable to learn.

  • Immersive Labs: Shared spatial learning for STEM, science, and advanced concepts.
  • Holographic Workstations: Tactile 3D manipulation for hands-on understanding.
  • Virtual Reality: Individual “presence-based” learning experiences for deep exploration.

02. Teacher Enablement & AI Support

At the core of the ecosystem is TASI, an AI co-pilot designed to support educators before, during, and after immersive sessions—reducing prep time and improving lesson delivery.

  • Content Generation: Lesson-aligned 3D environments and classroom materials.
  • Live Classroom Support: Guidance for teachers during immersive delivery.
  • Data-Driven Feedback: Insights into engagement and mastery over time.
  • Operational Automation: Reporting and routine admin support (where applicable).

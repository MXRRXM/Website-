Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Future Classroom?

A Future Classroom is a modern learning environment that combines immersive learning, XR education tools, AI teaching assistants, and advanced visual technologies to improve teaching and student engagement.

What is immersive learning?

Immersive learning uses technologies such as projection environments, virtual reality, holographic displays, and spatial computing to allow students to interact directly with educational content.

How does XR education work in schools?

XR education combines augmented, mixed, and virtual reality tools to help students visualize and explore complex subjects in a more engaging and memorable way than traditional teaching methods alone.

What is an AI teaching assistant?

An AI teaching assistant supports educators by generating lesson content, automating marking, providing insights, and helping align immersive experiences with curriculum objectives.

What is holographic learning?

Holographic learning allows students to interact with 3D educational content in real time, improving understanding of technical, scientific, and spatial subjects.
